Match Format Nova Scotia Youth Rules
Rally Scoring: A point is scored on every rally regardless of who served. Best 2-of-3 sets. Sets 1 & 2 played to 25, deciding Set 3 to 15. Win by 2, no cap.
Tripleball: Each athlete serves once before rotating. The scoresheet tracks the Serve-Toss-Toss sequence. Rotation happens after every serve regardless of rally outcome.
